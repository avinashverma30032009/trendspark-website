Why your brain keeps working while you sleep

Sleep is an active biological process. It supports learning, memory, attention and many systems that help your body function.

Sleep is not an off switch

Brain activity changes across sleep stages. The brain cycles through non-REM and REM sleep, each associated with different patterns of activity.

Memory and learning

Research suggests sleep helps stabilize and reorganize some memories. It is one reason a regular sleep schedule can support learning.

Build a steadier routine

Keep a consistent sleep and wake time where possible, make your room comfortable, and reduce late caffeine if it affects your sleep.

A useful caveat

Sleep needs vary by age and individual. Persistent sleep problems or severe daytime sleepiness deserve advice from a qualified health professional.
