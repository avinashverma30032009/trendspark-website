A simple 10-minute reset for a scattered day

When your attention is scattered, a small reset can help you choose a manageable next step instead of trying to fix the whole day at once.

TrendSpark Editorial · Practical guide

Minute 1–2: Clear one surface

Move distractions out of the way. You do not need to reorganize the whole room.

Minute 3–4: Capture open loops

Write down tasks and worries so you do not have to keep rehearsing them in your head.

Minute 5–6: Pick one priority

Choose a task that matters and can be started now. If it is large, define the first physical action.

Minute 7–10: Start small

Work on that first action for four minutes. Continue if momentum arrives; if not, you still have a clearer next step.
