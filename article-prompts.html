The simple prompt formula that gets clearer AI answers

Clear instructions often lead to more useful AI output. Try this four-part structure and refine it for your task.

TrendSpark Editorial · Practical guide

Role

Say what kind of helper you want, such as a patient maths tutor or an editor focused on plain language.

Context

Share the audience, goal, constraints and what you have already tried. Avoid including private or confidential information.

Task

Use a specific verb: compare, explain, outline, debug, summarize or critique.

Output format

Ask for a checklist, table, numbered steps or a short paragraph. Include length and tone if they matter.

Example template

You can start with: “Act as [role]. Context: [details]. Task: [specific request]. Return the answer as [format]. State assumptions and flag anything you cannot verify.”
