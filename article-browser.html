5 browser settings worth checking today

A few minutes spent reviewing browser settings can help you make more informed choices about privacy and security.

TrendSpark Editorial · Practical guide

1. Site permissions

Review which sites can access your location, camera, microphone and notifications. Remove permissions you no longer need.

2. Third-party cookies and tracking

Check your browser's privacy controls. Available protections and their effects vary by browser and website.

3. Saved passwords

Use a reputable password manager and unique passwords. Enable multifactor authentication for important accounts when available.

4. Browser updates

Turn on automatic updates where possible; updates often include security fixes.

5. Extensions

Remove extensions you do not use and review the permissions of those you keep. Install only from trusted sources.
